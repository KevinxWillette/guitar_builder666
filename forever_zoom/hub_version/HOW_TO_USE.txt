FOREVER ZOOM — one folder, two boards
=====================================

OPEN
  Double-click:  OPEN FOREVER ZOOM.bat
  That opens the INFINITE CANVAS (the old deep-zoom board).

BOARDS
  1) KILLY_ZOOMQUILT.html  — Infinite Canvas
     Scroll forever · pan · Draw (E) pencil/brush · My images for photos + short videos
     Right-click for tips. Videos loop as zoom layers (~41s fine).

  2) video-board.html — flat video board
     Free list of clips, pan/zoom, download project JSON.

  Hub: index.html (pick either)

DESK
  Only use this FOREVER ZOOM folder. Ignore empty KILLY_UNIVERSE_CANVASES on Desktop
  if it still exists — the live home is here.

TIPS (Infinite Canvas)
  Scroll     zoom deeper through layers
  Drag       pan
  E          draw / view (pencil tip on the layer)
  My images  load photos & videos
  M          main menu (if enabled)
